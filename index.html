What I help with

Health insurance
Cover for every stage of life. I explain waiting periods, sub-limits and exclusions before you buy.
  • Individual and family floater plans
  • Senior citizen health cover
  • Top-up and super top-up plans, including higher covers of 1 crore and above
  • Critical illness, cancer and heart cover
  • Newly married couples
  • Personal accident (individual and group)
  • Global coverage plans
  • Employee group health for your team
Life insurance
A simple way to protect the people who depend on you, with cover sized to your income and loans.
  • Term insurance, for earning members and homemakers
  • Term with return of premium
  • Pension plans
  • Endowment and whole life plans
  • Children's education plans
Motor insurance
Protection against accidents, theft and natural damage, renewed on time.
  • Private vehicles: car, SUV, bike
  • Commercial vehicles: taxi, school bus, trucks
  • Construction vehicles: tractor, tanker, tipper, hydra
  • Electric vehicles of all types
Commercial insurance
Cover for shops, offices and factories, reviewed against what you could lose.

  • Employee compensation (workmen's compensation)

Travel and home insurance
