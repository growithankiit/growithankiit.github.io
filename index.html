Questions people ask first

Does the policy review cost anything?

No. The review is free and you are under no obligation to buy anything.

How are you paid?

For insurance, the insurer pays me a commission when a policy is issued, and the premium you pay is the same as buying directly. For mutual funds, I distribute regular plans, where a distribution commission is built into the fund's expense ratio. Direct plans cost less and you can buy them yourself, and I will tell you that if you ask.

Do you work with one insurance company?

No. I can place policies with several insurers, so I compare options for your situation instead of offering one product.

I already have a policy. Can you still help?

Yes. Send me the policy and I will tell you in plain words what it covers and what it does not. I also help with renewals and with claims.

I do not live in Kolkata. Can we still work together?

Yes. I work with clients across India over phone and WhatsApp.

Can I pay my premium on this website?

No. Premiums are always paid directly to the insurer, through its official website, app or payment link, and never to me or through this site. Tell me which policy is due and I will send you the insurer's official link.
